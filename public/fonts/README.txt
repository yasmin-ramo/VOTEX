Fonts used by the MC 2026 voting app
====================================
standin-outfit-var.woff2        English stand-in for Nexa        (Outfit, SIL OFL 1.1)
standin-plex-arabic-*.woff2     Arabic stand-in for HelveticaNeue LT Arabic (IBM Plex Sans Arabic, SIL OFL 1.1)

Put the official Nexa and HelveticaNeue LT Arabic files here (as .woff2 for best results)
and follow the instructions at the top of /public/fonts.css.
